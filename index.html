PRIVACY NOTICE AND CONSENT FOR THE PROCESSING OF PERSONAL DATA

When you create your account at ronbo.nutri, you agree to the following:

1. Controller. ronbo.nutri is responsible for your data.

2. Data we collect. Name, email, phone, sex/gender, social media (optional), health condition declaration, food logs, weight and body composition, profile, progress and food photos (for nutritional analysis), and your nutrition goals.

3. Purpose. To provide personalized training and nutrition coaching services, track your progress, and communicate with you.

4. Storage. Your data is stored securely in Google Firebase (United States), with restricted access: only you and your trainer can see it.

5. Your rights. You may at any time request to view, correct, or delete your data. Deleting your account erases your records.

6. Retention. We keep your data while you are a client and up to 2 years after your last activity, unless you request deletion sooner.

7. Minors. This service is for adults 18 and older only.

8. Revocation. You may withdraw your consent at any time by writing to your trainer; doing so will delete your data and close your account.

9. Health and exercise. The trainer is an ISSA-certified fitness professional, not a medical professional. Consult your doctor before starting any exercise or nutrition program, especially if you declared a health condition.

By checking the box and creating your account, you declare that you read this notice and consent to the processing of your data for the purposes described.

Analyze a meal from a photo

📷 Before you take the photo: food photos are NOT saved. A photo is used only for this analysis, in this moment — the app keeps the meal record (the foods and their values), never the picture. If you want to keep the photo, keep it in your phone's gallery as well.

Daily analysis limit: photo and text analyses share a daily counter (a complete analysis usually takes 2 requests: identify + calculate). If your account has a daily limit set and you reach it, photo and text analysis pause until the counter resets the next day — you can still log every meal by hand. If no limit is set for your account, a default of 10 requests per day applies — enough for five complete meals (2 requests each).

🆕 Trainer account approvals

Each trainer may approve new accounts up to this limit. Approving an account assigns it to the trainer who approves it. If no limit is set, trainers cannot approve — only you can.

🎟️ Trainer memberships

A membership sets how many active students a trainer may have (Basic 5 · Premium 10 · Elite 30). Trainers request a level; only you approve it. This is separate from the account-approval limit above.

🍽️ Meal access confirmations

Meal logging is off for new students until requested. The trainer approves first; you confirm. Confirming turns meal logging on for that student.

🔀 Modality confirmations

A modality change applies only when you confirm it, after the trainer approved it. If the student has no trainer, their request reaches you directly and you approve and confirm it yourself.

📚 Exercise library · administrator

The library that feeds the plan editor. Everyone reads it; trainers contribute new exercises and the administrator approves them before they appear for everyone. Edits apply to the library itself — plans already saved keep their own copies.
