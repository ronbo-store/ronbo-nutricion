PRIVACY NOTICE AND CONSENT FOR THE PROCESSING OF PERSONAL DATA

When you create your account at ronbo.nutri, you agree to the following:

1. Controller. ronbo.nutri is responsible for your data.

2. Data we collect. Name, email, phone, sex/gender, social media (optional), health condition declaration, food logs, weight and body composition, profile, progress and food photos (for nutritional analysis), and your nutrition goals.

3. Purpose. To provide personalized training and nutrition coaching services, track your progress, and communicate with you.

4. Storage. Your data is stored securely in Google Firebase (United States), with restricted access: only you and your trainer can see it.

5. Your rights. You may at any time request to view, correct, or delete your data. Deleting your account erases your records.

6. Retention. We keep your data while you are a client and up to 2 years after your last activity, unless you request deletion sooner.

7. Minors. This service is for adults 18 and older only.

8. Revocation. You may withdraw your consent at any time by writing to your trainer; doing so will delete your data and close your account.

9. Health and exercise. The trainer is an ISSA-certified fitness professional, not a medical professional. Consult your doctor before starting any exercise or nutrition program, especially if you declared a health condition.

By checking the box and creating your account, you declare that you read this notice and consent to the processing of your data for the purposes described.
